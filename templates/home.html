Can I earn real money in this pilot?

No. Participation is voluntary testing with test XLM and test USDC. These tokens have no monetary value.

Does connecting my wallet move money?

No. Connecting establishes a signing session. A transfer requires a separate approval in your wallet.

Who handles a disagreement?

Authorised staff review the assignment evidence. A permitted staff wallet must sign the settlement, which can only send funds to the agreed participant wallets.

Is Push ready for mainnet?

No. Complete wallet acceptance testing and independent security review remain outstanding. Mainnet and real money payments are not part of this pilot.
