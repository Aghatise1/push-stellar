Straight answers

Current position
Is Push live?

No. This is a private development build. Accounts and workflows function locally; production payments and public launch controls are not enabled.

Does Push have a token?

No. Push does not require or issue a proprietary token.

Is Push tied to one blockchain?

No final chain exclusivity has been chosen. Stellar is used for the current testnet payment proof. Other integrations must solve a specific product need.

Does Push hold money?

No. The prototype is non-custodial and does not provide escrow. Production custody or escrow would require legal, operational and security work.

What is the platform fee?

Undecided. The original concept proposed 12.5%, but that is now a candidate to test rather than a published promise.

Who is building it?

Push is founded by Aghatise. A full public biography and verified contact details should be added before launch.
