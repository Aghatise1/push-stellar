Push Privacy Notice.

This notice explains the information used by the Push development preview and the choices available to its users.

1. Information we collect

Push stores account details such as your name, email address and authentication records; optional profile and public Stellar address information; and content you submit, including job listings, proposals, agreements, messages, delivery evidence, disputes and reviews.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, provide the work marketplace and private workrooms, send service emails, maintain transaction and moderation records, prevent abuse, troubleshoot the service and understand basic product activity.

3. Google sign-in

When you choose Google sign-in, Google provides Push with the account identity and email information needed to create or access your Push account. Push does not receive your Google password.

4. Stellar information

Public Stellar addresses, transaction hashes and testnet settlement references may be checked against the public Stellar test network. Blockchain records are public and may remain available independently of Push.

5. Service providers

Push currently uses Render for application hosting, Supabase for its PostgreSQL database, Google for authentication, Brevo for transactional email, and Stellar testnet services for public transaction verification. These providers process information as needed to operate their respective services.

6. Sharing and visibility

Public profiles and listings are visible only where the product identifies them as public. Proposals, workroom messages and delivery records are limited to the relevant participants and authorised staff handling support, safety or disputes. Push does not sell personal information.

7. Retention and security

We retain information while an account or work record is needed to provide the service, meet legitimate safety and record-keeping needs, resolve disputes or comply with law. We use access controls, encrypted connections and protected service credentials, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may update profile information in your account. To request access, correction or deletion of other personal information, contact Push using the address below. Some records may need to be retained for security, dispute or legal reasons.

9. Children

Push is not intended for children under 18 and does not knowingly invite them to create accounts.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this notice as the product changes and will state the new effective date here. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to aghatiseehioghae644@gmail.com.

This preview notice should be reviewed by a qualified privacy professional before a public commercial launch.
