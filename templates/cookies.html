Push Cookie Policy.

Push uses necessary cookies to secure account features. Optional cookies remain disabled unless you expressly accept them.

Necessary cookies

A secure session cookie keeps you signed in. A CSRF cookie protects forms from unauthorised cross-site submissions. These are required for account features and cannot be disabled inside Push.

Your consent preference

Your browser stores whether you accepted or declined optional cookies. This preference is kept on your device and is not used to track you.

Optional cookies

The current preview does not set advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. If analytics or other optional tools are introduced, they must remain disabled until you select “Accept optional cookies”. Selecting “Decline optional cookies” keeps them disabled.

Controls

You can clear your saved preference, Push cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing account cookies signs you out and clearing the preference makes the cookie choice appear again.
