Push Cookie Policy.

Push currently uses only the cookies and local browser storage needed to keep the preview secure and remember simple interface choices.

The current preview does not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels or behavioural advertising. If that changes, this policy and the consent controls must be updated before those tools are enabled.
