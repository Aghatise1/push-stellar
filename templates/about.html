Independent Stellar testnet MVP

Push records real work—not investment promises.

Push is an independent product project being developed from Nigeria. It connects a freelance brief to its agreement, delivery, messages and settlement evidence so workers and hiring accounts share one clear record.

What Push does

Push lets invited testers post legitimate work, apply, preserve agreed terms, communicate, submit delivery evidence and record how an approved assignment was settled. The current blockchain flow uses non-custodial Stellar testnet transactions signed by the user’s own wallet.

What Push does not do

Push is not a paid-task scheme, investment programme, token sale, exchange, yield product or deposit service. It does not promise earnings or returns for watching adverts, completing surveys, recruiting other people or depositing money. Push has no token, and testnet assets have no monetary value.

Current status

The service is an invite-only MVP used to test freelance coordination and settlement records. Simulated payment entries do not move money. Mainnet and commercial payment processing will remain disabled until provider, legal, security and operational requirements are satisfied.

Transparency
