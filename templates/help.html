How Push works.

A plain-language guide to jobs, agreements, delivery, payments and disputes.

01

Post a job

Describe the outcome, exact deliverables, acceptance checks, budget, deadline and included revisions. Ordinary jobs publish immediately; wording that suggests unsafe off-platform payment is held for Trust & Support review.

02

Select a worker

Applications remain private. Selecting someone creates a locked agreement snapshot that preserves the terms they considered.

03

Do the work

Use My work to open the assignment. Keep questions and changes in Messages so both people share the same record.

04

Submit delivery

The worker adds notes, a deliverable link and evidence. The client review deadline begins immediately.

05

Approve or revise

The client checks delivery against the acceptance criteria. Revisions count against the agreed limit.

06

Open a dispute

A dispute pauses normal release. A Trust & Support specialist reviews the agreement, messages and evidence before recording a decision.

Need help with an account or dispute?

Use the relevant workroom first so the evidence stays attached to the job. Trust & Support staff handle member questions, safety reports and disputes from one protected workspace.
