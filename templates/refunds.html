Testnet policy · 6 October 2026

Push Refund Policy.

The present invite-only preview does not charge platform fees, process bank or card payments, or offer real-money settlement.

Historical simulated records

Old bank, card and generic USDC simulation entries are historical demonstrations, not new payment options. No money moves, so they cannot be refunded.

Stellar testnet

Testnet assets have no monetary value. Final transactions cannot simply be reversed. While tokens remain in escrow, the contract permits an overdue-delivery refund or an authorised dispute settlement. Once released, those tokens are no longer held by the contract.

Disputes

Users may open a dispute on an active assignment. For funded escrow, an authorised staff wallet can sign a release, refund or split to the agreed parties after reviewing evidence. The record updates only after testnet confirmation. This moves test tokens, not real money.

Before real payments launch

Push must publish provider-specific fees, cancellation rules, refund timing and appeal procedures before enabling commercial payments.
