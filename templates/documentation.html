The wallet and escrow system

Push keeps agreements and delivery evidence in the workroom. Stellar testnet records contract deposits and settlement. Messages stay in the application; blockchain addresses and transactions are public.

Connection is a signing session

Connect the wallet matching your agreement in Freighter on testnet. Disconnect ends the Push signing session. A saved address alone does not mean your wallet is connected.

Ownership is saved evidence

Staff ownership verification records a signed proof. It can remain verified after disconnect. It does not grant contract authority.

Permission is separate

Staff need an approved role and the required contract permission. The owner grants administrator authority; administrators can manage reviewers. App suspension and chain revocation are separate actions.

People approve transfers

Agreement acceptance does not debit a wallet. The client signs funding and routine release. Staff only sign disputed settlements, restricted to the agreed participant wallets.

From agreement to settlement

  1. Agree the deliverables, asset, amount and deadlines with separate participant wallets.
  2. The client approves the testnet deposit. Wait for confirmation before beginning work.
  3. The worker submits delivery evidence. The client approves release or requests a permitted revision.
  4. If disputed, authorised staff review the evidence and sign a release, refund or split. Expiry paths also require a signed transaction.

Test XLM and test USDC have no monetary value. A pending request is not a confirmed payment. Push never asks for a recovery phrase or private key.

Current limits and technical evidence

The contract implementation has recorded testnet QA evidence. Complete Freighter acceptance and independent security review remain outstanding. Mainnet, bank payments, staff compensation and platform fees are not part of this release.
