# Push implementation review — 28 September 2026

## Delivered in this review

- Compact staff dashboards: four headline counts, a dated daily trend, actionable queues, period comparisons and supporting figures. Existing Push colours retained.
- Total members, new accounts today, active members today, and a five-minute online estimate. Staff are excluded from member counts.
- 7/30/90-day reporting, comparison against the preceding equal period, labelled chart axes and an expandable table of exact figures.
- Owner/administrator: member growth, jobs, assignments, completions, pipeline and separated testnet/simulated settlements. Platform revenue is explicitly unavailable, not invented.
- Trust & Support: one combined workspace for tickets, member investigations, reported listings, disputes, invitations and satisfaction reporting.
- New daily activity recording, with no historical visits invented. No IP addresses or browsing history are recorded by this feature.
- Owner report previews preserve the real role. Other staff cannot select another role's analytics. Duplicate dashboard controls and oversized chart decoration removed.
- Login and password-recovery throttling retain a styled form. Recovery delivery failures now appear in the email audit log without exposing whether an account exists.

## Existing implementation checked by regression tests

- Separate approved staff login and role dashboards for Owner, Administrator and Trust & Support; owner-only switching; staff documentation stays in Operations.
- Staff settings and sign-out; sign-out invalidates the session.
- Tester invitation creation, copy and revocation, including Trust & Support staff; invitations are single-use and email-bound.
- Admin approval of Trust & Support staff, restrictions and lifting restrictions with recorded reasons; protected owner/admin permissions.
- Registration, verification, reset flows and Google HTTPS callback generation behind Render.
- Job discovery by another member, private workrooms, profile photo/resume protection, disputes and payment verification rules.
- Support tickets, member appeals and satisfaction submissions with permission checks.

## Verification and its limits

92 automated tests passed locally with an isolated database. Desktop and 390px phone layouts were inspected, including chart labels, queue links and no horizontal page overflow. Browser error log was empty during this review. Tests use mocked external payment/email responses where appropriate; they do not prove a real inbox received mail or a wallet signed a real testnet transaction.

## Still requires external setup or a separate product decision

- Real email delivery: configure a verified Brevo sender and PUSH_BREVO_API_KEY on Render. The HTTPS backend is implemented; Gmail SMTP on Render Free remains unsuitable. Confirm both verification and password recovery with real inbox tests afterwards.
- Google sign-in: automated callback and account-handling checks pass. A complete Google account sign-in still needs an interactive end-to-end check against the production OAuth configuration.
- Staff compensation, platform fees and real-money custody/refunds are not implemented. Settlement records must not be described as revenue or money held by Push.
- Separate staging, tested backup restoration, CDN media migration and AI-provider activation are not certified by this review.
- Deleting old live test accounts needs an identified list of disposable records. This review's sample data is isolated locally and is not uploaded to production.

## Data definitions

Active means an authenticated HTML page request, recorded at most once per minute per session and once per account/day. Background notification polling and anonymous visits do not count. Online means a recorded request within five minutes, not an open websocket or guaranteed live connection. Historical activity starts at deployment. Report dates use the application timezone displayed in the report. Completion counts use settlement record dates, not assignment creation dates. Demo jobs are excluded. Satisfaction means ratings of 4 or 5 divided by submitted ratings, with the sample size shown.
