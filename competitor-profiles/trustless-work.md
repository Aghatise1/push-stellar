## Positioning and capabilities

Trustless Work presents reusable Stellar escrow infrastructure through a REST API. Its documentation covers deployment, funding, milestones, approval, disputes, resolution and release, with single-release and multi-release contract types and published testnet and mainnet endpoints.

## Strength relative to Push

It offers escrow as infrastructure that other products can integrate and already documents mainnet access. Push must not claim that contract escrow roles or milestone release are unique.

## Push distinction to test

Push combines the freelance work lifecycle with agreement-bound escrow: jobs, applications, preserved scope, private workrooms, delivery evidence, revisions and dispute context. This is a positioning hypothesis until supported by user evidence.
