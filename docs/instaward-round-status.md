# Instaward round status for Push

Checked 12 November 2026.

## Decision

Push should apply only through a verified Stellar Nigeria Instaward round. No currently open public Stellar Nigeria application form was found during this review. The November form previously used by Push was operated by Stellar Chile and must not be reused.

## Evidence

1. Tellus Cooperative emailed the founder on 10 November 2026 that the November application was not selected mainly because it listed Stellar Nigeria in a Stellar Chile round. Tellus recommended applying through the founder's own chapter.
2. Stellar's official September and October 2026 Ambassador Program update identifies Stellar Nigeria, the Stellar Nigeria Builders Circle and Nigerian Instaward recipients from October.
3. The same official update identifies Levai Agbara as the person leading developer relations for Stellar Nigeria and running the Builders Circle.
4. No official public page found on 12 November announced a currently open Stellar Nigeria form, deadline or sprint start date.

Official programme update: https://stellar.org/blog/ecosystem/stellar-ambassador-program-september-october-2026

## Required next action

Request the current application link and deadline inside the Stellar Nigeria Builders Circle. Before completing the form, verify all four of the following on the form itself:

1. The operating chapter is Stellar Nigeria.
2. Push is eligible for that specific round.
3. The funding cap and sprint duration match the SOW.
4. The named builder, wallet and submission declarations are accurate.

Until those checks pass, the round is **not identified and submission is blocked**. This is safer than presenting a Chile or general SCF form as the Nigeria round.
